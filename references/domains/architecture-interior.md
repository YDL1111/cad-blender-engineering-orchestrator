# Architecture and interior

Use for dimensioned rooms, fit-out, furniture/cabinetry, openings, finishes, and coordinated presentation. Control building grid/origin, levels, wall/opening thickness and position, clear height, circulation/egress inputs, accessibility inputs, fixed furniture interfaces, and room/object IDs.

Review measurable clearances, door/window operation, furniture use zones, headroom, level alignment, penetrations, and drawing/model correspondence. Treat building-code conclusions, structural adequacy, and professional sign-off as external unless authoritative project rules and qualified review are supplied.
