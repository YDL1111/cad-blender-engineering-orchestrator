# Mechanical and product

Use for dimensioned parts, enclosures, equipment, furniture products, and assemblies that proceed from CAD to Blender. Control datums, part numbers/object IDs, materials when engineering-relevant, critical dimensions/tolerances, interfaces, fasteners, clearances, motion envelopes, and assembly hierarchy.

Check uniqueness, fit interfaces, interference results, missing constraints, orientation, and export/read-back fidelity. Presentation geometry may not replace manufacturing CAD. Do not claim tolerance-stack, fatigue, FEA, certification, or manufacturability approval unless separately evidenced.
