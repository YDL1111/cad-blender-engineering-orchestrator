# Facility layout

Use for factories, warehouses, shops, showrooms, and equipment layouts with controlled footprints and routes. Control site/building origin, zones, equipment envelopes, service and maintenance clearances, aisles, doors, loading interfaces, utilities, and object IDs.

Check footprint/clearance collisions, access routes, equipment orientation, service connections, and cross-model coordinates. Reviewer judgment covers workflow and maintainability. Regulatory, safety, process-capacity, and structural conclusions require authoritative criteria and qualified review.
