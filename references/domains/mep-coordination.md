# MEP coordination

Use for physical routing and spatial coordination of plumbing, HVAC, electrical containment, and fire-protection objects. Control system/service, nominal/outer size, insulation allowance, elevations, slope direction/value, connections, fittings, equipment ports, access zones, and penetrations.

Deterministic checks may cover IDs, sizes, clearances, collisions, connectivity, and declared slopes. Reviewer judgment covers maintainability, routing quality, access, and unresolved discipline conflicts. Do not claim hydraulic, thermal, electrical, fire-code, CFD, or licensed design validation without separate authoritative calculations and review.
